# Block "pstrobe": phase-strobe chains A (PH_STROBE_P -> PSTRB_A) and K (PH_STROBE_N -> PSTRB_K)
Run `python3 gen_pstrobe.py [--norender]`. It writes `pstrobe.kicad_pcb`, `pstrobe.ifaces.json`, `pstrobe.png` (F red / B blue) and `pstrobe_detail.png` (GVA-84+, output column and clamp of chain A). KiCad 7.0.11. kicad-cli 7 has no DRC or ERC, so the checks below are boardlib's scripted ones.
**Size: 17 x 25 mm at board (9.5, 25.0). FLOORPLAN gave 17 x 23 at (9.5, 26.0); the block grew 1 mm at the north and 1 mm at the south.** The extra height is needed because the GVA-84+ (SOT-89) cannot be mirrored. Its GND tab always lies north of the line, 3.95 mm from it: in A it points away from the axis and in K towards it. The 13.2 mm stub must clear that tab in K. The phase corridor (board y 34.5-40.5) stays free. The phase band at board y 25-26 is empty there, and board y 49-50 is free.
Chain (each): 47 R -> 100 nF -> TRF37D73 -> FB on the line + 47 uH 1210 || 1 k + cold-pad 100 nF -> 100 nF -> 10 dB pi pad -> 100 nF -> GVA-84+ -> FB (pad on the corner) + 2.2 uH 0805 to VBUS -> 100 nF -> launch node: TL 80 ps shorted stub + DNP BAT15 clamp -> output.

## Deviations
- **Mirror symmetry**: every 2-pin part, track, via, stub and tee is generated by `chain(s)` and mirrored about y = 12.5. The TRF37D73 (rotation 0) and the GVA-84+ (rotation 90) are not mirrored, because their pin-outs are chiral. Their RF pins sit on the mirrored line points, so the electrical paths are identical.
- **Bias tees on B.Cu**: the bead (pad 1 centred on the line) stays on top. A via at bead pad 2 leads to a straight chain on B.Cu: choke (pad over the via) -> cold-pad 100 nF -> supply via pair at the capacitor pad. The 1 k sits beside the 1210. There was no room on top next to the stub. The vias are behind the bead, outside the RF path.
- **C432 / C433**: the analog block used C403 / C408 as its cold-end capacitors, which left C432 / C433 unplaced. They are placed here as spare +3V3 100 nF on B.Cu at the TRF VCC via pairs.
- **Local helpers**: `bplace` (B-side placement; Block.place(side="B") segfaults), `two`, `snap`, `decap`, and `render` (shifts the crop by the board bbox). boardlib.py is not edited.
- **GVA-84+ datasheet**: minicircuits.com returned 403, so it was not checked online. The netlist footprint SOT-89-3 assumes pin 1 RF in, pin 2 / tab GND, pin 3 RF out + DC, the standard DF782 pin-out. The tab has 4 GND vias. Bias: VBUS -> bead -> 2.2 uH, no resistor (Vd ≈ VBUS - 0.15 V).
## Open points
- The stub keep-out (2 w) of the A / K stubs reaches 0.9 mm into the phase corridor (board y 34.5-35.4 and 39.6-40.5). The phase block has no copper there, but the integrator must keep the F.Cu GND pour out of it.
- The clamp pad sits 0.12 mm from the stub copper. As a DNP part, it only adds pad capacitance (a few fF).
- Check the GVA land against the Mini-Circuits PL drawing and the VBUS 4.75 V headroom (sim/bias_tee). PH_STROBE P/N skew is matched up to the block edges; matching beyond them is the integrator's job.

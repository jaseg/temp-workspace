## Placement rationale
- **J101** SIG_IN is an edge-launch SMA on the left edge. In1/In2 are cut out under the 1.27 mm centre pad, and a 0.5 mm step tapers into the 0.17 mm line. GND vias surround it.
- **K101 HFD3 is on the bottom side.** Its pin rows lie 2.54 mm above and below the VIN line, with COM 4/9 at x = 13.4. This keeps the top side above the relay body free, so R101/R102 (100R 0603, one per pole) sit between the rows. Each stub runs VIN tap -> R -> COM pin, about 4.4 mm per pole including the resistor body. RESULT asks for < 2 mm, which the THT pin pitch does not allow; each pole's 100R is in series with that stub. NO pins 5/8 go to GND through the planes. Coil: VBUS -> R103 39R 0805 -> pin 1; pin 12 -> TERM_50.
- **HF divider.** C101 Ctop (1.5p) is 1.1 mm after the taps. GA connects to C102 Cbot (3.3p) and R104 Rg (10R). The IN trace (0.15 mm, 2.4 mm) runs to U101 pin 2. R105/R106 (2 x 4.99M) go to In_Bias pin 3.
- **Low-C cut-out** (rule area, x 16.0-20.9, y 13.4-14.95; starts at Ctop pad 2, so VIN keeps its GND): no copper on F.Cu pour, In1, In2 or B.Cu pour under Ctop pad 2, GA, Cbot pad 1, Rg, IN, R105 pad 1 and U101 pin 2.
  - Guard: the In_Bias trace (bootstrapped, follows IN) runs beside IN. No other net comes within 0.3 mm. The B.Cu traces stay out of the cut-out.
- **U101 BUF802 RGT.** The KiCad VQFN-16 3x3 EP 1.68 land pattern matches TI RGT0016: 0.5 mm pitch, 1.68 mm EP. The EP is internally isolated and tied to GND with 4 vias of 0.2 mm drill (tent or plug them).
  - Decoupling 100n: VS+ C108, VSO+ C117, VS- C110/C111, VSO- C118 (bottom, under the pin-10 via). Bulk: C115 (+5V) and C116 (-5V), 10u each.
  - R_Bias R107 17.8k; Caux C107; Raux R121.
  - CLH divider R108/R109/C103 and CLL divider R110/R111/C104 sit above the IC.
- **Servo.** The first 300k (R114) sits on the VIN line between the relay columns. The string R115/R116 leads to TAP, then R117 100k and C105 Cp. U102 OPA197 has C106 Cint, R118 Rf1 (sensed through a via at the OUT pin and a B.Cu trace), R119 Rofs, R120 + RV101 Bourns 3314J trimmer, and TP103 SERVO.
  - OPA_OUT reaches Raux on B.Cu through the channel between the relay pin columns.
  - OPA V+/V- caps C112/C113 are on the bottom side.
- **Output.** OUT pin 11 connects to the edge pad IF1 (**BUF_OUT_S**, right edge, y = 14.0, 1.6 mm from the pin). The sampler's 10R must start within about 1.4 mm beyond the edge (3 mm from the pin).
  - R112 49.9R sits 0.9 mm from the pin, then R113 0R (series position). The 50R microstrip runs to IF2 (**BUF_OUT_T**, bottom edge, x = 24.75), with GND stitching vias at 0.85 mm spacing.

## Interface points (silkscreen labels)
- IF1 BUF_OUT_S: right edge, top copper. Abut the sampler fragment here.
- IF2 BUF_OUT_T: bottom edge. Continue as a 0.17 mm microstrip over In1 GND to TMUX136.
- J2 (right edge, 1.27 mm header): GND, +5V, +2V5, -2V5, VOFS.
- J3 (bottom-left, 1.27 mm header): GND, -5V, TERM_50, VBUS.
- During integration, replace the headers with board traces. +5V/-5V can come straight from the In2 planes.
